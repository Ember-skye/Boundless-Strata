## Overview

Boundless Strata is a 2D sandbox survival game. Dig, build, craft, and explore a vast procedurally generated world spanning seven distinct biomes, from icy tundra to dense jungle to sun-scorched desert — each with its own terrain, trees, and atmosphere. Survive the night, manage your hunger and thirst, gear up in crafted armor, and carve out your own space in a world that keeps expanding the further you explore.

---

## Controls

| Key / Input | Action |
|---|---|
| `A` / `D` / `←` / `→` | Move left / right |
| `W` / `Space` / `↑` | Jump |
| `Left Click` (hold) | Mine, attack, place blocks, or collect items — context-based on what you're holding |
| `Left Click` (double-click) | Eat/drink selected item; fill a bottle at water |
| `Right Click` | Pick up & swap inventory items / arm an item throw |
| `1`–`9`, `0` | Select hotbar slot |
| `Mouse Wheel` | Cycle hotbar slots |
| `E` | Open/close Inventory & Crafting |
| `TAB` | Open/close World Map |
| `R` | Respawn |
| `T` | Skip forward in time |
| `F` | Eat selected food |
| `F1` | Toggle debug overlay |
| `F5` | Quick save |
| `F11` | Toggle fullscreen |
| `F12` | Toggle Creative Mode |
| `ESC` | Pause menu / close panels |

Holding a **block** and left-clicking places it; holding a **tool or weapon** and left-clicking mines or attacks instead. Right-clicking a stackable item arms a throw — left-click in the world to launch it toward your cursor.

## World & Exploration

The world is enormous — far larger than you'll fully explore in one playthrough — and is divided into seven biomes running from one edge to the other:

- 🌊 **Sea** — coastal water at the world's edges
- ❄️ **Tundra** — snow, ice patches, and pine forest
- 🌾 **Grassland** — rolling hills, flowers, and oak trees
- 🌲 **Forest** — denser woodland with giant trees
- 🌴 **Jungle** — tall canopies, vines, and mud
- 🏜️ **Savanna** — dry grass and dead, twisted trees
- 🏝️ **Desert** — sand, cacti, and sandstone

Biomes blend smoothly into one another rather than cutting off abruptly, so transitions feel natural as you travel. Underground, you'll find progressively tougher stone layers the deeper you dig — from ordinary stone near the surface down through limestone, granite, basalt, and obsidian, eventually reaching unbreakable bedrock. Ores are scattered throughout, with rarer and more valuable ores found only at greater depths.

Caves, ravines, and rare floating islands add variety to exploration, and lakes, ponds, and underground lava pools are scattered throughout the world. Water behaves dynamically — it flows, fills basins, and can be redirected by digging or dammed by building.

## Day & Night Cycle

A full day cycles through night, dawn, day, and dusk. Enemies come out at night and clear away by morning; animals are most active during the day. If you'd rather skip the wait, sleeping in a bed lets you pass through the night — or rest during the day to fast-forward to evening.

---

## Survival

You'll need to manage three stats to stay alive:

- ❤️ **Health** — lost from enemy attacks, hunger, dehydration, or lava. Restored by eating food.
- 🍗 **Hunger** — drains over time (faster while running). Left unmanaged, starvation will start damaging you.
- 💧 **Thirst** — drains steadily; refills quickly while swimming, or restore it directly with a filled water bottle. Dehydration also deals damage if ignored.

Swimming slows your fall and changes how you move through water. Lava, on the other hand, is dangerous — expect steady damage and reduced control if you fall in.

You start with a basic pickaxe, sword, some torches, and enough building materials to get your first shelter up before nightfall.

---

## Combat

Weapons and tools double as your means of defense. Swords range from a basic Wooden Sword up through Stone, Iron, Gold, and finally Diamond — each hitting harder and lasting longer than the last. A Bow and Arrows offer ranged options for enemies you'd rather not get close to.

Landing hits knocks enemies back, and you'll get a brief window of invulnerability after taking a hit yourself, so smart positioning matters as much as gear.

---

## Armor

Craft armor at the Anvil across five tiers — Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, and Diamond — each with a Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots. Equipped armor reduces the damage you take from enemies, so gearing up a full set is one of the best ways to push into more dangerous territory.

---

## Crafting & Building

Progression revolves around a handful of crafting stations, each unlocking deeper tiers of gear:

- **Basic Crafting** (no station needed) — simple early-game items
- **Workbench** — the backbone of early crafting
- **Furnace** — smelt ores into usable metal
- **Campfire** — cook food
- **Anvil** — forge weapons and armor

Chests give you storage for anything you don't want cluttering your inventory, and beds let you set a respawn point (and skip time).

---

## Animals & Enemies

Peaceful animals roam the surface during the day, while hostile enemies like slimes and zombies come out after dark and need to be fought off or avoided. Defeating enemies and gathering resources both feed into your crafting progression.

---

## Interface

- **Inventory & Crafting** — manage your items and craft what you have materials for
- **Creative Inventory** — (Creative Mode only) full access to every item in the game, organized by category
- **World Map** — a zoomable, pannable overview of everywhere you've explored
- **Tooltips** — hover any item to see its stats, tier, and durability
- **Debug Overlay** — optional on-screen info for the curious (FPS, coordinates, time of day, and more)

---

## Saving Your Game

Your progress saves automatically at key points, and you can trigger a manual Quick Save at any time. You can always return to the main menu to load a different world without closing the game.
